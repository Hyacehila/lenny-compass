# Future public blog outline

Working title: **How I turned a large product-and-startup archive into a personal decision system**

1. The problem: saved links are not a usable knowledge base.
2. The boundary: a paid archive can support private learning, not public redistribution.
3. The map: eight practical themes and cross-cutting filters for stage, role, and business model.
4. The workflow: import, validate, tag, select anchor pieces, retrieve evidence, and write notes.
5. The tool: a local-only search and reading workbench with evidence-grounded answers.
6. The lesson: optimize for repeatable decisions, not for reading every document.

The public post may include original diagrams, methodology, small title-level examples, and links to official sources. It must not include archive text, reconstructed full-text search data, or a public query interface.
